Green Walk Plus

Everything you need to record and keep walks is free. Plus is optional and unlocks your full walk history (free shows the 30 most recent — older walks are never deleted, just hidden until you upgrade), 24-hour and 7-day forecasts (free: 6 hours and 3 days), more saved places, extra map styles and GPX export.

Weekly

An auto-renewing subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically each week unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.

Lifetime

A single one-time purchase. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. It stays yours on every iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

Prices are shown in your local currency in the app before you buy.

Cancel the weekly subscription

Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → Green Walk → Cancel Subscription. Plus stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore a purchase

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open the app → Settings → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Plus with.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple, so Apple handles refunds — we can't issue them ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Questions

Why does recording stop when I lock my phone?

Green Walk needs location access set to Always to keep recording in your pocket: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Wander → Always. It is only used while a walk is in progress.

Why does my route look jagged?

GPS is less accurate between tall buildings, under trees and indoors. The live screen shows the current signal quality so you know when to expect it. Routes are usually smoother in open areas.

Why wasn't my walk saved?

Very short walks — a few seconds or a few metres — aren't kept, so an accidental tap on Start doesn't fill up your history.

My walk was interrupted. Did I lose it?

No. If a call, a flat battery or a force quit stops a walk, Green Walk offers to carry on from where it stopped, or to save what it recorded, the next time you open it.

Does my location leave my phone?

Your routes never do — they're stored only on your iPhone. To show the weather, air quality and places, the app sends a coordinate (with no identity attached) to the services that answer those questions. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I move my walks to a new iPhone?

Not in version 1.0 — walks stay on the iPhone that recorded them. With Plus you can export any walk as a GPX file to keep a copy. Your Plus purchase itself moves with you via Restore Purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Delete any walk from its detail screen, or delete the app to remove everything. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to ask us to erase.

Where does the weather come from?

Forecasts and air quality come from Open-Meteo. Map data © Apple Maps and © OpenStreetMap contributors.
